A multi-platform campaign built to drive real intent

Frosty & Mills sought a creative and strategic marketing partner capable of producing a high-quality, multi-platform commercial campaign to be aired on TV, YouTube, and social media. The aim was to elevate brand visibility, deepen audience connection, and drive meaningful online action.

Empixa led the full project cycle — from strategic analysis and production planning to final editing and digital campaign execution — backed by a performance-driven media strategy.

Scope of Work

  • Analysed past customer behaviour and developed multiple buyer personas
  • Shaped concepts with the production team to match the brand's personality
  • Managed the full shoot — props, wardrobe, styling and location setup
  • Delivered high-quality visuals optimised for every channel
  • Executed a full-funnel campaign — remarketing, landing pages, offers and email flows
